In Chapter 1 of The Great Catsby, narrator Nick Carraway moves to West Egg, Long Island, in 1922 to work in the bond business. He reconnects with his cousin, Daisy Buchanan, and her wealthy husband, Tom, in East Egg, meeting golfer Jordan Baker. The chapter establishes the superficiality of the elite, Tom’s infidelity, and Nick’s observation of his mysterious neighbor, Jay Catsby, reaching toward a green light
